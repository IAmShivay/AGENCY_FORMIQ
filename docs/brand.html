Brand guidelines · v1.0 · November 2026

Formiq

01 Logo

Symbol and wordmark

The logo reads Formiq. Its capital F is the symbol: three modules on a 3×3 grid, with the top arm ending in a quarter circle. "ormiq" is constructed monoline lettering: every letter is drawn from one stroke weight, circles and straight lines, not typed in a font. One element joins them: the spark, an Ember square that is both the F's middle arm and the dot of the i. It is the part of the logo that never changes.

Reversed: on Ink or photography

02 Symbol

The F and the spark

For favicons, app icons, social avatars, the condensed nav and documents. The F sits on a 3×3 grid: stem, top arm, and the spark as its middle arm.

Default: Ink tile

Ember tile: campaigns and social only

Small sizes: 48, 32 and 16 px. The spark still reads at 16 px.

03 Space and size

Clear space and minimum size

Leave clear space on every side equal to one grid module of the F (a third of its height). The full lockup needs at least 96 px of width on screen (25 mm in print). Below that, use the symbol.

05 Motion

How the logo moves

The logo builds itself on every page load: the F's stem rises, the top arm slides out, the quarter circle swings into place and the spark drops in with a quarter-turn. Then "ormiq" draws on stroke by stroke, like a pen, and the i's spark lands last. On hover the F's spark turns through a diamond and the i's spark hops. On a first visit the loading screen goes further: the 3×3 construction grid draws first and the F snaps together on it; then the letters un-draw, the F folds away, its spark stays and grows into an Ember screen, and that screen lifts away to reveal the site. Every movement uses the same ease (expo out), and reduced-motion settings turn all of it off.

06 Misuse

Keep it intact

  • Don't recolour the spark or round its corners. It is always a square, always Ember (Paper on an Ember tile).
  • Don't put a second f after the symbol, or retype the lettering in a font. The F is the first letter. In running text, write Formiq (legal name: Formiq Studio).
  • Don't stretch it, outline it, add shadows or gradients, or set the wordmark in another font.
  • Don't place the primary logo on Ember or busy imagery. Use the reversed logo on Ink.
  • Don't change the spacing between the F and "ormiq", or stack them. They are one word.
